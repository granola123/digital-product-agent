What counts as a real comparable

  • Same delivery format — PDF + Notion (or at least digital, instant-download), not a physical planner.
  • Similar scope — a themed weekly plan / recipe bundle, not a blank all-purpose planner.
  • Has visible traction — carries a sales count or review number, so the price is proven, not aspirational.
  • Comparable design tier — professional layout, not a single clip-art page — matches what this bundle looks like.
  • Recent — shop has activity in the last few months, so pricing reflects the current market, not 2021 rates.
