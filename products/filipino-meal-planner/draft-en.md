## Grocery List (by category)

**Produce & Aromatics:** garlic, onion, fresh ginger, tomatoes, radish (labanos), eggplant, long green chilies (siling pangsigang), Thai chilies (siling labuyo), string beans (sitaw), kangkong (water spinach, or substitute spinach), green papaya (or chayote), malunggay leaves (or substitute baby spinach), carrots, cabbage, snow peas (sitsaro), potato, red bell pepper, green bell pepper, green onion (scallion), calamansi or lime

**Protein:** chicken thighs and drumsticks (bone-in, skin-on), pork belly, pork ribs, pork shoulder (thin-sliced for pancit), ground pork, beef chuck, shrimp, eggs

**Rice, Noodles & Wrappers:** jasmine or steamed white rice, dried pancit canton noodles (wheat noodles), lumpia/spring roll wrappers

**Canned, Dairy & Coconut:** coconut milk, condensed milk, evaporated milk, liver spread (e.g. Reno-style), tomato sauce, shrimp paste (bagoong alamang), quickmelt cheese (optional), pitted green olives

**Sauces & Pantry:** soy sauce, white cane vinegar (sukang maasim), fish sauce (patis), oyster sauce, bay leaves, whole peppercorns, brown sugar, white sugar, vanilla extract, tamarind soup base or tamarind paste, cooking oil, sweet chili sauce or banana ketchup (for dipping)

## Recipe Cards

All ingredient tables below are scaled for 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 servings.

### 1. Chicken Adobo
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken thighs/drumsticks, bone-in | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Soy sauce | 20ml | 40ml | 60ml | 80ml |
| White cane vinegar | 20ml | 40ml | 60ml | 80ml |
| Garlic cloves, crushed | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Bay leaves | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Whole peppercorns | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Brown sugar | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Water | 60ml | 120ml | 180ml | 240ml |
| Cooking oil | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Steamed rice (to serve) | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |

**Steps:**
1. Combine the chicken, soy sauce, and garlic in a bowl or zip-top bag and let it marinate 15–30 minutes (ask an adult to help handle raw chicken safely and wash your hands right after).
2. Ask an adult to help heat the oil in a wide pot over medium-high heat. Lift the chicken out of the marinade (keep the marinade) and sear it skin-side down 3–4 minutes until golden, then flip — this is just for color, not to cook it through yet.
3. Pour in the reserved marinade, plus the vinegar, water, bay leaves, peppercorns, and sugar.
4. Bring to a boil uncovered and let it bubble, without stirring, for 3 minutes — this cooks off the vinegar's sharp raw smell. Stirring too soon traps that harshness in the sauce instead.
5. Cover, reduce the heat to a gentle simmer, and cook 25–30 minutes until the chicken is fork-tender and there's no pink left near the bone.
6. Uncover, raise the heat slightly, and simmer 5–8 more minutes until the sauce reduces to a thick, glossy glaze that coats the back of a spoon.
7. Taste and adjust the salty-sour-sweet balance with a touch more sugar or vinegar if needed.
8. Serve hot over steamed rice, spooning the extra glaze over the top.

### 2. Pork Sinigang (Sour Tamarind Soup)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pork belly or ribs, cubed | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Water | 500ml | 1000ml | 1500ml | 2000ml |
| Onion, quartered | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Tomato, quartered | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Tamarind soup base (or tamarind paste) | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Radish (labanos), sliced | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Eggplant, sliced | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Long green chili (siling pangsigang) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| String beans (sitaw), cut | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Kangkong or spinach | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Fish sauce (patis) | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |

**Steps:**
1. Combine the pork, water, onion, and tomato in a large pot. Ask an adult to help bring it to a boil, skimming off the grey foam that rises to the top so the broth stays clear.
2. Reduce to a simmer, cover partway, and cook 40–45 minutes until the pork is tender when pierced with a fork.
3. Stir in the tamarind soup base (or tamarind paste) until fully dissolved — the broth should turn cloudy and taste distinctly sour.
4. Add the radish and eggplant and simmer 5 minutes until just starting to soften.
5. Add the string beans and green chili, and simmer 3–4 minutes more.
6. Season with fish sauce to taste, tasting carefully since it's hot.
7. Stir in the kangkong or spinach last and cook just 1 minute until wilted — adding leafy greens too early turns them dull and mushy.
8. Ladle into bowls and serve hot with steamed rice, extra fish sauce, and calamansi on the side.

### 3. Pancit Canton (Stir-Fried Noodles)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pancit canton noodles (dried) | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Pork, thinly sliced | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Shrimp, peeled | 50g | 100g | 150g | 200g |
| Garlic, minced | 1 clove | 2 cloves | 3 cloves | 4 cloves |
| Onion, sliced | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Cabbage, shredded | 50g | 100g | 150g | 200g |
| Carrot, julienned | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Snow peas (sitsaro) | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Chicken broth | 100ml | 200ml | 300ml | 400ml |
| Soy sauce | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Oyster sauce | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Cooking oil | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Calamansi or lime wedges | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

**Steps:**
1. Soak the dried pancit canton noodles in warm water 5–10 minutes until pliable, then drain (skip this if your noodles are pre-softened fresh ones).
2. Ask an adult to help heat the oil in a wok or large pan over medium-high heat. Sauté the garlic and onion 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
3. Add the pork and cook 2–3 minutes until no longer pink, then add the shrimp and cook 1–2 minutes until opaque and curled.
4. Pour in the chicken broth, soy sauce, and oyster sauce, and bring to a simmer.
5. Add the carrot and cook 2 minutes, then add the cabbage and snow peas and cook 2 more minutes until crisp-tender.
6. Add the drained noodles and toss gently to coat, 3–5 minutes, letting them soak up the liquid — add a splash more broth if they look dry before they're fully tender.
7. Season with black pepper to taste.
8. Serve hot with calamansi or lime wedges squeezed over the top just before eating.

### 4. Chicken Tinola
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken thighs/drumsticks, cut up | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Fresh ginger, sliced | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Garlic cloves, crushed | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Onion, sliced | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Green papaya (or chayote), sliced | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Malunggay leaves (or baby spinach) | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Fish sauce (patis) | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Water or chicken broth | 300ml | 600ml | 900ml | 1200ml |
| Cooking oil | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |

**Steps:**
1. Ask an adult to help heat the oil in a pot over medium heat. Sauté the ginger 1–2 minutes until fragrant, then add the garlic and onion and cook 2 minutes until softened.
2. Add the chicken pieces and cook 3–4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned on the outside.
3. Pour in the fish sauce, stir for 30 seconds, then add the water or broth.
4. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer, cover partway, and cook 20–25 minutes until the chicken is tender.
5. Add the green papaya or chayote and simmer 5–8 minutes until just tender, not mushy.
6. Stir in the malunggay or spinach leaves and simmer 1–2 minutes until wilted.
7. Taste and add a little more fish sauce if the broth needs more savoriness.
8. Ladle into bowls and serve hot with steamed rice alongside.

### 5. Bicol Express (Spicy Pork in Coconut Milk)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pork belly, sliced or cubed | 130g | 260g | 390g | 520g |
| Coconut milk | 100ml | 200ml | 300ml | 400ml |
| Shrimp paste (bagoong alamang) | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Garlic, minced | 1 clove | 2 cloves | 3 cloves | 4 cloves |
| Onion, chopped | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Fresh ginger, minced | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Long green chilies, sliced | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Thai chilies (siling labuyo), sliced | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Cooking oil | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |

**Steps:**
1. Ask an adult to help heat the oil in a pot over medium heat. Sauté the garlic, onion, and ginger 2–3 minutes until fragrant and the onion turns translucent.
2. Add the pork and cook 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned at the edges.
3. Stir in the shrimp paste and cook 1 minute until it darkens slightly and smells toasty rather than raw.
4. Pour in the coconut milk, stirring to combine into a pale orange sauce.
5. Bring to a gentle simmer — don't let it boil hard, or the coconut milk can curdle — and cook uncovered 25–30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the pork is tender and the sauce has thickened and released a little oil at the edges.
6. Add the long green chilies and simmer 5 minutes until slightly softened.
7. Stir in the Thai chilies for the final 2 minutes, adjusting the amount to how spicy you'd like it.
8. Taste carefully (it's hot!) and serve over steamed rice.

### 6. Beef Caldereta
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beef chuck, cubed | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Liver spread | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Tomato sauce | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Beef broth or water | 150ml | 300ml | 450ml | 600ml |
| Potato, cubed | 50g | 100g | 150g | 200g |
| Carrot, sliced | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Red bell pepper, sliced | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Green bell pepper, sliced | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Pitted green olives, sliced | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Onion, chopped | 25g | 50g | 75g | 100g |
| Garlic, minced | 1 clove | 2 cloves | 3 cloves | 4 cloves |
| Bay leaf | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Quickmelt cheese, shredded (optional) | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Cooking oil | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |

**Steps:**
1. Ask an adult to help heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat. Brown the beef in batches, 3–4 minutes per side, until deeply browned all over (don't crowd the pot, or the beef steams instead of browning). Set the beef aside.
2. In the same pot, sauté the onion and garlic 2–3 minutes until softened.
3. Return the beef to the pot and add the tomato sauce, broth, and bay leaf. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer.
4. Cover partway and simmer 60–75 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the beef is fork-tender.
5. Add the potato and carrot and simmer 10–12 minutes until just tender.
6. Stir in the liver spread until it fully dissolves into the sauce, thickening and darkening it.
7. Add the bell peppers and green olives, and simmer 5 minutes until just softened, not mushy.
8. Stir in the shredded cheese, if using, until melted and the sauce turns glossy.
9. Taste and season with salt, or a touch more liver spread for richness.
10. Serve hot over steamed rice.

### 7. Lumpia Shanghai (Fried Spring Rolls)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground pork | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Carrot, finely minced | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Green onion, minced | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Garlic, minced | 1 clove | 2 cloves | 3 cloves | 4 cloves |
| Egg (binder + sealing wrappers) | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Soy sauce | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Salt & pepper | pinch | pinch | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp |
| Lumpia/spring roll wrappers | 8 | 16 | 24 | 32 |
| Oil, for frying | 250ml | 500ml | 750ml | 1000ml |
| Steamed rice (to serve) | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Sweet chili sauce or banana ketchup | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |

**Steps:**
1. In a bowl, combine the ground pork, carrot, green onion, garlic, soy sauce, salt, and pepper. Mix with your hands in a circular motion until it feels slightly sticky and elastic — this is what keeps the filling from falling apart when fried.
2. Lay a wrapper on a clean surface in a diamond orientation and spoon a small log of filling near the bottom corner.
3. Fold the bottom corner over the filling, fold in both sides, then roll tightly away from you into a snug cylinder.
4. Dab a little beaten egg on the top corner to seal the roll shut. Repeat with the remaining wrappers and filling.
5. Ask an adult to help heat the oil in a deep pot to about 175°C (350°F) — a small piece of wrapper dropped in should sizzle steadily, not too violently.
6. Fry the rolls in batches of 4–5, turning occasionally, 4–5 minutes until deep golden brown and crisp all over. This step needs an adult, since hot oil can splatter and burn.
7. Drain on paper towels or a wire rack.
8. Slice the larger rolls in half on a diagonal if you like, and serve hot over steamed rice with sweet chili sauce or banana ketchup for dipping.

### 8. Leche Flan (Bonus)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Egg yolks | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 |
| Condensed milk | 60ml | 120ml | 180ml | 240ml |
| Evaporated milk | 60ml | 120ml | 180ml | 240ml |
| Vanilla extract | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Calamansi or lemon zest (optional) | pinch | pinch | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp |
| Sugar (for caramel) | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Water (for caramel) | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |

**Steps:**
1. Ask an adult to help melt the sugar with the water in a llanera (oval flan mold) or small cake pan over medium heat, swirling gently, until it turns a deep amber color — watch closely, since sugar can go from amber to burnt in seconds.
2. Carefully tilt and swirl the mold to coat the bottom evenly with the caramel, then set it aside to harden while you make the custard.
3. In a bowl, whisk the egg yolks gently — just enough to break them up, not until foamy, since bubbles leave holes in the finished flan.
4. Stir in the condensed milk, evaporated milk, vanilla extract, and zest (if using), mixing gently until smooth.
5. Strain the mixture through a fine sieve into the mold, pouring it over the hardened caramel, to catch any bits of egg membrane or bubbles.
6. Cover the mold tightly with foil.
7. Ask an adult to help set the mold in a steamer over gently simmering (not vigorously boiling) water, and steam 25–30 minutes, until the custard is set but still slightly jiggly in the center.
8. Let the flan cool to room temperature, then refrigerate at least 4 hours (overnight is best) so it fully firms up and chills.
9. To unmold, run a thin knife around the edge, place a serving plate on top, and flip quickly so the caramel sauce pools over the custard.
10. Slice and serve chilled.
