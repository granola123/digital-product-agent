## Recipe Cards

### 1. Chicken Souvlaki with Tzatziki

**Chicken & Marinade**
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken thighs, boneless | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Olive oil | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Lemon juice | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Garlic (cloves) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Dried oregano | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |
| Salt & pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp |
| Pita bread | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Cherry tomatoes | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 |
| Red onion (thin slices) | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |

**Tzatziki**
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Greek yogurt | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Cucumber, grated | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Garlic (cloves) | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Fresh dill, chopped | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | 1 tbsp | 4 tsp |
| Olive oil | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Lemon juice | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |

**Steps:**
1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized chunks, about the size of a large postage stamp — this helps them cook evenly and thread easily onto skewers. Ask an adult to help if you're still learning to use a knife safely.
2. In a bowl, mix the olive oil, lemon juice, crushed garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper. Add the chicken chunks and stir until every piece is coated. Cover and let it marinate in the fridge at least 30 minutes — the longer it sits (even a few hours), the more flavor soaks in.
3. While the chicken marinates, make the tzatziki: grate the cucumber, then squeeze small handfuls of it hard over the sink to wring out as much water as you can. This step matters — skipping it makes your tzatziki watery instead of thick and creamy.
4. Stir the squeezed cucumber into the Greek yogurt along with the crushed garlic, chopped dill, olive oil, and lemon juice. Taste and add a pinch of salt if needed. Cover and refrigerate until serving.
5. If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for 15 minutes so they don't catch fire on the grill. Thread the marinated chicken pieces onto the skewers, leaving a little space between each piece so they cook evenly on all sides.
6. Ask an adult to help heat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high heat until it's hot enough that a drop of water sizzles on contact.
7. Lay the skewers in the pan and cook 3–4 minutes per side without moving them too much, turning every few minutes, until every side has golden-brown grill marks.
8. Check doneness by cutting into the thickest piece of chicken — it should be completely white inside with no pink, and the juices should run clear, not pink.
9. Warm the pita bread for about 20 seconds per side in the same pan, just until it's soft and slightly puffed.
10. Build your plate: warm pita, souvlaki skewers, a generous scoop of tzatziki, cherry tomatoes, and red onion slices. Squeeze extra lemon over the top if you like it bright and tangy.

### 2. Avgolemono Soup (Greek Lemon Chicken & Rice Soup)

| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken breast | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Chicken stock | 300ml | 600ml | 900ml | 1200ml |
| Short/medium-grain rice | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Eggs | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Lemon juice | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Salt & pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp |
| Fresh dill, chopped (garnish) | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |

**Steps:**
1. Ask an adult to help you pour the chicken stock into a pot and bring it to a boil over medium-high heat — you'll see steady bubbles rising from the bottom.
2. Add the chicken breast whole (don't cut it up yet) and turn the heat down so the stock is gently simmering, just lazy little bubbles, not a hard boil. Cook 15 minutes, until the chicken is no longer pink in the middle when you cut into the thickest part.
3. Lift the cooked chicken out onto a plate with tongs and set it aside to cool slightly. Keep the pot of stock simmering on the stove.
4. Add the rice to the simmering stock and cook 12–15 minutes, until the grains are soft all the way through when you bite one, not chalky in the center.
5. While the rice cooks, shred the cooled chicken into bite-sized pieces with two forks or your fingers.
6. This next part is the trickiest — and the most important — step, so go slowly: crack the eggs into a heatproof bowl and whisk them well. Slowly whisk in the lemon juice.
7. Now, ladle about one cup of the hot soup broth into the egg-lemon mixture in a thin, slow stream while whisking constantly. This is called "tempering" — it slowly warms the eggs so they don't scramble into lumps when they hit the hot pot.
8. Turn the heat down to low. Slowly pour the warmed egg mixture back into the pot, stirring constantly the whole time.
9. Add the shredded chicken back in and stir gently for 1–2 minutes over low heat until the soup turns pale yellow and slightly thickened, like a light custard-y broth. Don't let it boil now, or the eggs will curdle into little scrambled bits.
10. Season with salt and pepper to taste, ladle into bowls, and sprinkle with fresh dill before serving.

### 3. Kotopoulo Lemonato (Greek Lemon Chicken & Potatoes)

| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken thighs, bone-in | 200g | 400g | 600g | 800g |
| Potatoes, cut into wedges | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Olive oil | 20ml | 40ml | 60ml | 80ml |
| Lemon juice | 30ml | 60ml | 90ml | 120ml |
| Lemon zest | ½ lemon | 1 lemon | 1½ lemons | 2 lemons |
| Garlic (cloves) | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Dried oregano | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | 1 tbsp | 4 tsp |
| Chicken stock or water | 60ml | 120ml | 180ml | 240ml |
| Salt & pepper | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |

**Steps:**
1. Ask an adult to help you preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C) — this dish needs high heat to turn the potatoes golden.
2. In a large baking dish, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, crushed garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper.
3. Add the chicken thighs and potato wedges to the dish and toss everything with your hands (or two spoons, if you don't want lemony, garlicky fingers) until well coated.
4. Arrange the chicken skin-side up and spread the potatoes in a single layer around it so everything has room to brown instead of steam.
5. Pour the chicken stock or water around the edges of the dish — not over the chicken skin, so it stays crisp.
6. Ask an adult to help place the dish in the oven. Roast uncovered for 45–55 minutes.
7. Halfway through, carefully use oven mitts to pull the dish out and spoon some of the pan juices back over the chicken and potatoes — this is called basting, and it keeps everything from drying out.
8. Check the chicken is done by cutting into the thickest piece near the bone: the meat should be completely white with clear juices, no pink. A meat thermometer should read 165°F (74°C) if you have one.
9. The potatoes are ready when a fork slides in easily, like poking a soft baked potato, and the edges look golden and slightly crispy.
10. Let everything rest 5 minutes out of the oven before serving so the juices settle back into the meat instead of running out onto the plate.

### 4. Psari Plaki (Baked Lemon-Garlic Fish with Tomatoes)

| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| White fish fillets (cod, halibut, or sea bass) | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Tomatoes, diced (or canned) | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Onion, thinly sliced | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Zucchini or bell pepper, sliced | 50g | 100g | 150g | 200g |
| Garlic (cloves) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Olive oil | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Lemon juice | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Fresh parsley, chopped | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | 1 tbsp | 4 tsp |
| Salt & pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp |

**Steps:**
1. Ask an adult to help you preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Ask an adult to help heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and cook, stirring occasionally, for 4–5 minutes until it turns soft and slightly see-through, not brown.
3. Add the garlic and cook just 30 seconds more, stirring constantly, until you can smell it — garlic burns fast, so don't walk away.
4. Add the diced tomatoes and sliced zucchini or bell pepper. Simmer 5 minutes, stirring now and then, until the tomatoes break down into a chunky sauce.
5. Pat the fish fillets dry with a paper towel and season both sides lightly with salt and pepper. Lay them in a baking dish.
6. Pour the tomato-vegetable sauce over the fish, spreading it so every piece is mostly covered. Drizzle the lemon juice over the top.
7. Cover the dish tightly with foil — this traps steam so the fish cooks gently and stays moist instead of drying out.
8. Ask an adult to help place the dish in the oven. Bake covered for 15 minutes, then carefully remove the foil (steam will rush out, so open it away from your face) and bake 5–8 minutes more, uncovered.
9. Check doneness by gently pressing a fork into the thickest part of a fillet and twisting slightly — the fish should flake apart easily into soft white flakes and look opaque all the way through, not glassy or translucent.
10. Sprinkle with fresh parsley and serve straight from the baking dish, spooning extra sauce over each portion.

### 5. Pastitsio (Baked Pasta with Meat Sauce & Béchamel)

**Meat Sauce & Pasta**
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bucatini or penne pasta | 80g | 160g | 240g | 320g |
| Ground beef | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Onion, chopped | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Garlic (cloves) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Crushed tomatoes (canned) | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Tomato paste | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | 1 tbsp | 4 tsp |
| Ground cinnamon | pinch | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Olive oil | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Salt & pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp |

**Béchamel Topping**
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unsalted butter | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| All-purpose flour | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Whole milk | 125ml | 250ml | 375ml | 500ml |
| Egg | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| Ground nutmeg | pinch | pinch | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp |
| Grated kefalotyri or parmesan | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |

**Steps:**
1. Ask an adult to help bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta 2 minutes less than the package says — it will finish cooking in the oven, so slightly firm (al dente) is exactly right. Drain and set aside.
2. Ask an adult to help heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook 4–5 minutes, stirring, until soft and see-through.
3. Add the garlic and ground beef. Break the beef apart with your spatula as it cooks, like crumbling cookie dough, for 5–6 minutes until there's no pink left.
4. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, cinnamon, salt, and pepper. Turn the heat down so it's gently simmering and cook uncovered 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens and doesn't look watery anymore.
5. While the sauce simmers, make the béchamel: ask an adult to help melt the butter in a separate saucepan over medium-low heat. Whisk in the flour and cook, whisking constantly, for 1–2 minutes until it smells slightly toasty and looks like wet sand — this cooked flour-butter paste is called a roux.
6. Slowly pour in the milk while whisking constantly, a little at a time at first, so no lumps form. Keep whisking over medium-low heat for 5–7 minutes until the sauce coats the back of a spoon and holds a line when you drag your finger through it.
7. Take the béchamel off the heat and let it cool for 2 minutes (so it doesn't cook the egg into scramble). Whisk in the egg, nutmeg, and grated cheese until smooth.
8. Ask an adult to help preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Spread half the cooked pasta in a greased baking dish, layer the meat sauce over it, then top with the remaining pasta.
9. Pour the béchamel evenly over the top, using a spatula to spread it all the way to the edges so every bite gets some.
10. Ask an adult to help place the dish in the oven. Bake 30–35 minutes, until the top turns deep golden brown in spots and the sauce is bubbling gently at the edges.
11. Let the pastitsio rest at least 15 minutes before cutting — this is important, since cutting it too soon means it falls apart instead of holding neat squares. Cut into squares and serve.

### 6. Moussaka (Layered Eggplant & Meat Bake)

**Vegetables & Meat Sauce**
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Eggplant, sliced into rounds | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Potato, sliced into rounds | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Ground beef or lamb | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Onion, chopped | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Garlic (cloves) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Crushed tomatoes (canned) | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Tomato paste | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | 1 tbsp | 4 tsp |
| Ground cinnamon | pinch | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Olive oil (for frying) | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Salt & pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp |

**Béchamel Topping**
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unsalted butter | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| All-purpose flour | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Whole milk | 125ml | 250ml | 375ml | 500ml |
| Egg | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| Ground nutmeg | pinch | pinch | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp |
| Grated kefalotyri or parmesan | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |

**Steps:**
1. Slice the eggplant and potato into rounds about as thick as a pencil. Lay the eggplant slices on a tray, sprinkle both sides with a little salt, and let them sit 20 minutes — this pulls out excess water and bitterness. Pat them dry with a paper towel afterward.
2. Ask an adult to help preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Brush the eggplant and potato slices with olive oil on both sides and arrange them on baking trays in a single layer, not overlapping.
3. Ask an adult to help place the trays in the oven. Roast 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway, until both are soft when poked with a fork and lightly golden at the edges. Roasting instead of pan-frying uses much less oil but gets you the same soft, silky texture.
4. While the vegetables roast, ask an adult to help you cook the onion in a splash of olive oil in a pan over medium heat for 4–5 minutes until soft.
5. Add the garlic and ground meat, breaking it apart with your spatula, and cook 5–6 minutes until no pink remains.
6. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, cinnamon, salt, and pepper. Simmer uncovered 10–15 minutes until thick, not watery.
7. Make the béchamel exactly as in the pastitsio recipe: melt butter, whisk in flour to make a roux, slowly whisk in milk until thickened and smooth, then cool slightly and whisk in the egg, nutmeg, and cheese.
8. In a greased baking dish, layer the roasted potatoes first, then the eggplant, then spread the meat sauce evenly over the top.
9. Pour the béchamel over everything, spreading it to the edges with a spatula so it forms a smooth, even blanket.
10. Ask an adult to help place the dish in the oven (still at 400°F/200°C) and bake 30–35 minutes, until the top is deep golden brown with some darker spots and the edges are bubbling.
11. Let it rest at least 20 minutes before cutting — moussaka is even softer and more delicate than pastitsio, and resting lets the layers firm up so your slice holds together instead of collapsing.

### 7. Gemista (Rice-Stuffed Tomatoes & Peppers)

| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Large tomatoes | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Bell peppers | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Short/medium-grain rice, uncooked | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Onion, finely chopped | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Garlic (cloves) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Fresh parsley, chopped | 1 tbsp | 2 tbsp | 3 tbsp | 4 tbsp |
| Fresh mint or dill, chopped | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | 1 tbsp | 4 tsp |
| Olive oil | 30ml | 60ml | 90ml | 120ml |
| Pine nuts (optional) | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Sugar | pinch | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Salt & pepper | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |

**Steps:**
1. Ask an adult to help you preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Slice the tops off the tomatoes and peppers like little lids and set the lids aside — you'll put them back on before baking. Ask an adult to help with this if you're still learning knife skills.
3. Using a spoon, scoop out the inside flesh and seeds of the tomatoes into a bowl (save this pulp — it goes into the filling). Scoop the seeds and white ribs out of the peppers and discard those.
4. Chop the reserved tomato pulp into small pieces with a knife or squeeze it through your fingers.
5. Ask an adult to help heat the olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook 4–5 minutes until soft, then add the garlic and cook 30 seconds more until fragrant.
6. Stir in the chopped tomato pulp, uncooked rice, parsley, mint or dill, pine nuts, sugar, salt, and pepper. Cook 2–3 minutes, stirring, just to coat the rice in the oil and let the flavors mix — the rice will still be hard and uncooked at this point, and that's correct, since it finishes cooking inside the vegetables in the oven.
7. Stand the hollowed tomatoes and peppers upright in a snug baking dish (they should lean against each other so they don't tip over). Spoon the rice filling into each one, filling only about three-quarters full — the rice needs room to expand and soak up juices as it cooks, so an overstuffed vegetable can split open.
8. Place the reserved lids back on top of each stuffed vegetable. Drizzle any remaining olive oil over everything and pour a splash of water into the bottom of the dish.
9. Cover the dish tightly with foil. Ask an adult to help place it in the oven and bake 45 minutes covered.
10. Carefully remove the foil (watch for escaping steam) and bake another 30–45 minutes uncovered, until the tomatoes and peppers look soft and slightly wrinkled and the rice inside is fully tender when you taste a bit — not crunchy.
11. Let the dish cool for 10 minutes before serving — gemista is traditionally served warm or at room temperature, not piping hot.

### 8. Baklava (Bonus)

| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phyllo dough sheets | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 |
| Walnuts, finely chopped | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Ground cinnamon | pinch | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Unsalted butter, melted | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Sugar | 25g | 50g | 75g | 100g |
| Water | 30ml | 60ml | 90ml | 120ml |
| Honey | 20ml | 40ml | 60ml | 80ml |
| Lemon juice | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Whole cloves (optional) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

**Steps:**
1. Take the phyllo dough out of the freezer and let it thaw in the fridge overnight, or on the counter for about an hour. Phyllo dries out and cracks fast once unwrapped, so keep the stack covered with a lightly damp kitchen towel while you work and only pull out one sheet at a time.
2. Ask an adult to help preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Brush a baking dish all over with melted butter.
3. In a bowl, mix the chopped walnuts with the cinnamon.
4. Lay one sheet of phyllo in the dish and brush it all over with melted butter using a pastry brush — get into the corners so it doesn't stay dry and papery. Repeat with half of your phyllo sheets, buttering each one before adding the next.
5. Sprinkle the walnut-cinnamon mixture evenly over this stack of buttered phyllo.
6. Layer the remaining phyllo sheets on top, brushing each one with butter just like before, finishing with a well-buttered top layer.
7. Ask an adult to help with this step, since it takes a steady hand with a sharp knife: cut all the way through the layers into diamond or square shapes before baking — it's much harder to cut cleanly after baking, once everything turns crisp.
8. Ask an adult to help place the dish in the oven. Bake 40–45 minutes, until the top is deep golden brown and the layers look crisp and flaky, not pale or soft.
9. While the baklava bakes, make the syrup: ask an adult to help combine the sugar, water, honey, lemon juice, and cloves (if using) in a small pot. Bring to a boil, then turn down to a simmer for 5–8 minutes, until it's slightly thickened, like warm maple syrup. Remove the cloves and let the syrup cool while the baklava finishes baking.
10. As soon as the baklava comes out of the oven — still hot — pour the cooled syrup evenly over the top. Pouring cool syrup over hot baklava (instead of the other way around) is the trick that keeps the layers crisp instead of turning soggy.
11. Let the baklava sit uncovered at room temperature for at least 4 hours, or overnight, so the syrup has time to soak all the way down through the layers. Serve at room temperature, cut along the lines you scored before baking.
