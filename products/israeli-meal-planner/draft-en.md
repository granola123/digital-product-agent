## Grocery List (by category)

**Produce & Herbs:** onions, red onion, garlic, red bell pepper, eggplant, tomatoes, cucumbers, lemons, fresh parsley, fresh cilantro, fresh chili (optional, for heat)

**Protein:** eggs, chicken breast, chicken thighs (boneless), chicken hearts, chicken livers, white fish fillets (cod, hake, or halibut), dried chickpeas (NOT canned — falafel needs dried, soaked chickpeas for a crisp texture)

**Grains, Legumes & Bread:** pita bread, white rice, panko breadcrumbs, all-purpose flour

**Dairy & Pantry Staples:** whole milk, feta cheese (optional, for shakshuka), tahini paste, hummus, amba (pickled mango sauce), pickles, canned crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, olive oil, vegetable oil (for frying)

**Spices & Flavorings:** ground cumin, sweet paprika, ground coriander, ground turmeric, ground cinnamon, caraway seeds, cayenne pepper or chili flakes, garlic powder, baking soda, sesame seeds, cornstarch, rosewater, granulated sugar, pomegranate syrup (optional, for malabi), salt, black pepper

## Recipe Cards

All ingredient tables below are scaled for 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 servings.

### 1. Shakshuka
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Olive oil | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Onion, diced | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Red bell pepper, diced | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Garlic cloves, minced | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Crushed tomatoes (canned) | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Tomato paste | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Ground cumin | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Sweet paprika | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Chili flakes | pinch | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp |
| Sugar | pinch | pinch | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp |
| Salt | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Eggs | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Feta cheese, crumbled (optional) | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Fresh parsley or cilantro, chopped | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Pita or crusty bread, to serve | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

**Steps:**
1. Ask an adult to help warm the olive oil in a wide skillet or pan that has a lid, over medium heat.
2. Add the diced onion and red bell pepper. Cook 5–6 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft and starting to turn translucent.
3. Add the garlic, cumin, paprika, and chili flakes. Stir for about 30 seconds, just until you can smell the spices — don't let the garlic brown or it turns bitter.
4. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, sugar, salt, and black pepper. Let it simmer, uncovered, for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens and no longer looks watery.
5. Carefully taste the sauce (it's hot!) and adjust the salt or chili to your liking.
6. Use the back of a spoon to make one small well in the sauce for each egg. Crack each egg into a small cup first, then gently slide it into a well — this keeps the yolk whole and keeps shell bits out.
7. Cover the pan, turn the heat down to low, and cook 5–8 minutes, until the egg whites are completely set but the yolks are still soft and jiggly in the middle (cook a few minutes longer if you like firm yolks).
8. Sprinkle with crumbled feta and chopped parsley or cilantro.
9. Serve the pan right at the table with warm pita or crusty bread for scooping up the eggs and sauce.

### 2. Sabich
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Eggplant, sliced into rounds | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Salt (for drawing out eggplant moisture) | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Vegetable oil, for frying | 45ml | 90ml | 135ml | 180ml |
| Eggs (for hard-boiling) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Pita bread | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Hummus | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Tomato, diced | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Cucumber, diced | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Lemon juice | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Tahini paste | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Water (to thin tahini) | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Garlic clove (for tahini sauce) | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Amba (pickled mango sauce) | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Pickles, sliced | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Black pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ½ tsp |

**Steps:**
1. Put the eggs in a small pot and cover with cold water. Ask an adult to help bring it to a boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook 9–10 minutes for fully hard-boiled eggs. Cool in a bowl of cold water, then peel and slice.
2. Slice the eggplant into rounds about ½ cm thick. Sprinkle both sides with salt and let them sit on paper towels for 10 minutes — this draws out extra moisture so the eggplant fries crisp instead of soggy. Pat dry before frying.
3. Ask an adult to help heat the oil in a large pan over medium-high heat until it looks shimmery.
4. Fry the eggplant in batches (don't crowd the pan, or the oil cools down and the eggplant turns greasy) for 2–3 minutes per side, until deep golden brown and soft all the way through. Drain on paper towels.
5. Make the tahini sauce: in a small bowl, whisk the tahini paste, water, lemon juice, garlic, and a pinch of salt until smooth and pourable. Add a little more water, a teaspoon at a time, if it's too thick.
6. Make the salad: toss the diced tomato and cucumber with a squeeze of lemon juice, salt, and black pepper.
7. Warm the pita in a dry pan or toaster until soft, then cut off the top to open the pocket.
8. Spread hummus inside the pita, then layer in the fried eggplant slices and sliced hard-boiled egg.
9. Top with the tomato-cucumber salad, a generous drizzle of tahini sauce, a spoonful of amba, and a few pickle slices.
10. Serve right away while the eggplant is still warm.

### 3. Israeli Chicken Schnitzel with Chopped Salad
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken breast, pounded thin | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| All-purpose flour | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Egg, beaten | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Panko breadcrumbs | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Sesame seeds | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | 3 tsp | 4 tsp |
| Garlic powder | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Sweet paprika | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Salt | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Vegetable oil, for frying | 45ml | 90ml | 135ml | 180ml |
| Tomato, diced | 50g | 100g | 150g | 200g |
| Cucumber, diced | 50g | 100g | 150g | 200g |
| Red onion, diced | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Lemon juice | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Olive oil (for salad) | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Lemon wedges, to serve | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

**Steps:**
1. Put the chicken breast between two sheets of plastic wrap. Ask an adult to help pound it with a meat mallet or rolling pin until it's an even ½cm thick — this helps it cook fast and stay juicy.
2. Set up 3 shallow bowls: one with flour, one with the beaten egg, and one with the panko mixed with sesame seeds, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper.
3. Dip the chicken in the flour first (shake off the extra), then the egg, then the breadcrumb mix, pressing gently so the crumbs stick all over.
4. Ask an adult to help heat the oil in a large pan over medium-high heat until it looks shimmery.
5. Fry the chicken 3–4 minutes per side, until deep golden brown and cooked through with no pink left in the middle.
6. Drain on paper towels.
7. Make the salad: toss the diced tomato, cucumber, and red onion with the lemon juice, olive oil, and a pinch of salt — chop everything as small as you can for a true Israeli chopped salad.
8. Serve the schnitzel hot with a big pile of chopped salad and lemon wedges for squeezing over the top.

### 4. Chicken Shawarma with Rice and Salads
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken thighs, boneless | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Olive oil | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Lemon juice | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Garlic cloves, minced | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Ground cumin | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Ground coriander | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Sweet paprika | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Ground turmeric | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Ground cinnamon | pinch | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp |
| Salt | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ½ tsp |
| White rice | 50g | 100g | 150g | 200g |
| Cucumber & tomato salad, diced | 50g | 100g | 150g | 200g |
| Tahini sauce, to serve | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |

**Steps:**
1. In a bowl, whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, cumin, coriander, paprika, turmeric, cinnamon, salt, and black pepper together into a marinade.
2. Add the chicken thighs and turn them to coat completely. Cover and marinate in the fridge at least 2 hours — overnight tastes even better, since the spices have more time to soak in.
3. Ask an adult to help heat a large pan or grill pan over medium-high heat.
4. Cook the chicken 6–7 minutes per side, until deeply browned outside and cooked through inside (cut into the thickest piece to check — no pink, and the juices should run clear).
5. Let the chicken rest 5 minutes on a cutting board, then slice it into thin strips — this is what makes it taste like real shawarma instead of just grilled chicken.
6. Meanwhile, cook the rice following the package directions.
7. Serve the sliced chicken over the rice with the cucumber-tomato salad on the side and a drizzle of tahini sauce over everything.

### 5. Falafel with Tahini Sauce and Pita
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dried chickpeas, soaked 12–24 hr | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Onion, chopped | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Garlic cloves | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Fresh parsley | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Fresh cilantro | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Ground cumin | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |
| Ground coriander | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |
| Salt | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Baking soda | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Vegetable oil, for frying | 250ml | 500ml | 750ml | 1000ml |
| Pita bread | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Tahini paste (for sauce) | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Water (to thin tahini sauce) | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Lemon juice (for tahini sauce) | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Tomato & cucumber, diced, to serve | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |

**Steps:**
1. Drain the soaked chickpeas well and pat them as dry as you can. Do not cook them first — using raw, soaked (not canned) chickpeas is exactly what makes falafel crisp on the outside instead of mushy.
2. In a food processor, pulse the chickpeas, onion, garlic, parsley, and cilantro until it looks like coarse, pebbly crumbs — not a smooth paste. Ask an adult to help if you haven't used a food processor before.
3. Add the cumin, coriander, salt, and black pepper. Pulse a few more times just to combine everything evenly.
4. Scrape the mixture into a bowl, cover, and refrigerate at least 1 hour — this helps the falafel hold together instead of falling apart in the oil.
5. Right before frying, sprinkle the baking soda over the mixture and mix it in — it makes the falafel light and fluffy on the inside.
6. Scoop and shape the mixture into small balls or patties, about the size of a golf ball.
7. Ask an adult to help heat the oil in a deep, heavy pot over medium heat until it's hot enough for frying — test with one falafel; it should sizzle right away and rise toward the surface within a few seconds.
8. Fry in small batches for 3–4 minutes, turning occasionally, until deep golden brown and crisp all over. Drain on paper towels.
9. Make the tahini sauce: whisk the tahini paste, water, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt together until smooth and pourable.
10. Warm the pitas, split them open, and tuck in the falafel, diced tomato and cucumber, and a generous drizzle of tahini sauce.

### 6. Jerusalem Mixed Grill
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken thigh, diced | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Chicken hearts, trimmed and diced | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Chicken livers, diced | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Onion, sliced | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Garlic cloves, minced | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Olive oil | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Ground cumin | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Ground turmeric | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Ground coriander | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Salt | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Pita bread | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Tahini sauce, to serve | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Fresh parsley, chopped | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |

**Steps:**
1. Trim the chicken hearts (cut off any tough tops) and pat the livers dry with paper towels. Cut the hearts, livers, and thigh meat into small, bite-size pieces, roughly the same size, so everything cooks evenly.
2. Ask an adult to help heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add the sliced onion and cook 4–5 minutes, until soft and starting to turn golden at the edges.
4. Add the garlic, cumin, turmeric, coriander, salt, and black pepper. Stir for about 30 seconds, just until fragrant.
5. Add the chicken thigh pieces first, since they take the longest to cook. Cook 4–5 minutes, stirring, until no pink remains.
6. Add the chicken hearts and cook 3 more minutes.
7. Add the livers last — they cook fastest and turn tough and chalky if overcooked. Cook just 2–3 minutes, until browned outside but still a little rosy-tan (not grey) in the middle.
8. Taste a piece to check the seasoning and add a little more salt if needed.
9. Warm the pita, spread tahini sauce inside, pile in the mixed grill, and sprinkle with chopped parsley.

### 7. Chraime
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| White fish fillets (cod, hake, or halibut) | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Olive oil | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Garlic cloves | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 |
| Sweet paprika | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | 3 tsp | 4 tsp |
| Ground cumin | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |
| Caraway seeds | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Cayenne pepper or chili flakes | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Tomato paste | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Water or fish stock | 100ml | 200ml | 300ml | 400ml |
| Lemon juice | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Salt | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Fresh cilantro, chopped | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |

**Steps:**
1. Ask an adult to help make the spice paste: in a small bowl, mash together the garlic, paprika, cumin, caraway, and cayenne with a splash of the olive oil until it forms a thick, fragrant paste.
2. Warm the rest of the olive oil in a wide, shallow pot over medium heat.
3. Add the spice paste and cook, stirring, for about 1 minute, just until it smells toasty — don't let the garlic burn or the whole dish will taste bitter.
4. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 1 more minute, until it darkens slightly.
5. Pour in the water or fish stock and the lemon juice, stir well, and bring to a gentle simmer.
6. Season with the salt, then carefully taste (it's hot!) — the sauce should taste spicy, garlicky, and a little tangy.
7. Nestle the fish fillets into the sauce in a single layer, spooning a little sauce over the top of each piece.
8. Cover and simmer gently for 10–12 minutes, until the fish flakes easily with a fork and looks opaque (no longer see-through) all the way through. Try not to stir once the fish is in, or it can break apart.
9. Sprinkle with the chopped cilantro and serve warm, straight from the pot, with bread or rice to soak up the sauce.

### 8. Malabi (Bonus)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whole milk | 200ml | 400ml | 600ml | 800ml |
| Sugar (for pudding) | 25g | 50g | 75g | 100g |
| Cornstarch | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Rosewater (for pudding) | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |
| Sugar (for syrup) | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Water (for syrup) | 30ml | 60ml | 90ml | 120ml |
| Rosewater or pomegranate syrup (for topping) | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Chopped pistachios | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Shredded coconut (optional) | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |

**Steps:**
1. In a small bowl, whisk about a quarter of the milk together with the cornstarch until completely smooth, with no lumps — this is called a slurry.
2. Ask an adult to help warm the rest of the milk with the sugar in a saucepan over medium heat, stirring until the sugar fully dissolves. Don't let it boil yet.
3. Slowly pour the cornstarch slurry into the warm milk, whisking constantly so it doesn't clump.
4. Keep whisking over medium heat for 3–5 minutes, until the mixture thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon, like a thin pudding.
5. Take the pot off the heat and stir in the rosewater — adding it off the heat keeps its scent bright instead of cooking it away.
6. Pour into small cups or glasses and let cool at room temperature, then refrigerate at least 3 hours, until fully set and chilled.
7. Meanwhile, make the syrup: warm the sugar and water in a small pot over low heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves, then stir in the rosewater or pomegranate syrup (pomegranate syrup gives a tarter, fruity, deep red version) and let it cool.
8. Right before serving, drizzle the cooled syrup over each pudding and sprinkle generously with chopped pistachios (and shredded coconut, if using).
