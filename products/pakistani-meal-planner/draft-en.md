## Recipe Cards

All ingredient tables below are scaled for 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 servings.

### 1. Chicken Karahi
A restaurant-style curry that comes together fast in a wide, heavy pan: bone-in chicken seared hard, then simmered down with ripe tomatoes, ginger, and green chilies until the masala turns thick, glossy, and clinging — no watery gravy here, just deep, smoky, tomato-forward heat finished with a bright snow of julienned ginger.

| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken, bone-in pieces (skinless) | 200g | 400g | 600g | 800g |
| Ghee or vegetable oil | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Tomatoes, chopped | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Ginger, minced | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |
| Garlic, minced | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |
| Green chilies, slit | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Red chili powder | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Ground cumin | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Ground coriander | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Salt | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Ginger, julienned (garnish) | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Fresh cilantro, chopped | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Roti or naan, to serve | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

**Steps:**
1. Ask an adult to help warm the ghee in a wide, heavy skillet or karahi over medium-high heat.
2. Add the chicken pieces and sear 5–6 minutes, turning occasionally, until browned on all sides (it doesn't need to cook through yet — that happens later in the sauce).
3. Add the minced ginger and garlic and stir for about 30 seconds, just until fragrant.
4. Add the chopped tomatoes, red chili powder, cumin, coriander, and salt. Stir well, then cover and cook 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes break down into a thick sauce.
5. Uncover and add the slit green chilies. Keep cooking uncovered over medium-high heat for 8–10 minutes, stirring often, until the oil starts to separate and shine at the edges of the pan and the masala looks thick and glossy rather than watery — this step is called bhuna, and it's what gives karahi its deep, concentrated flavor.
6. Check a piece of chicken near the bone to make sure it's cooked through with no pink; simmer a few minutes longer if needed.
7. Season with the black pepper and taste, adjusting salt if needed.
8. Scatter the julienned ginger and chopped cilantro over the top right before serving.
9. Serve straight from the pan with warm roti or naan for scooping.

### 2. Chicken Biryani
Layers of saffron-gold basmati rice and deeply spiced chicken, steamed together under a tight lid until the two halves swap perfume — the rice drinking in the meat's smoky masala, the chicken picking up whispers of cardamom and rose-gold saffron steam. Crisp fried onions and fresh mint scattered on top finish it like confetti.

| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basmati rice | 75g | 150g | 225g | 300g |
| Chicken, bone-in pieces | 200g | 400g | 600g | 800g |
| Plain yogurt | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Ginger-garlic paste | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | 3 tsp | 4 tsp |
| Onion, thinly sliced | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Oil or ghee | 20ml | 40ml | 60ml | 80ml |
| Tomato, chopped | 50g | 100g | 150g | 200g |
| Green chilies, slit | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Red chili powder | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Ground turmeric | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Garam masala | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Cinnamon stick | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Green cardamom pods | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Whole cloves | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Bay leaf | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Fresh mint, chopped | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Fresh cilantro, chopped | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Saffron strands | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 |
| Milk (to bloom saffron) | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Salt | ¾ tsp | 1½ tsp | 2¼ tsp | 3 tsp |
| Lemon juice | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |

**Steps:**
1. Rinse the basmati rice under cold water until it runs clear, then soak it in a bowl of water for 30 minutes. Drain well.
2. In a bowl, mix the chicken with the yogurt, half the ginger-garlic paste, the red chili powder, turmeric, and a pinch of the salt. Set aside to marinate while you prep everything else.
3. Ask an adult to help warm half the oil in a wide pot over medium heat. Add the sliced onion and fry, stirring often, for 8–10 minutes until deep golden brown and crisp — this is the birista; scoop it out onto a paper towel, keeping the flavored oil in the pot.
4. Add the remaining ginger-garlic paste and cook 30 seconds until fragrant, then add the chopped tomato and green chilies. Cook 5 minutes until the tomato softens.
5. Add the marinated chicken to the pot along with the garam masala and remaining salt. Cook, stirring, for 8–10 minutes until the chicken is mostly cooked through and coated in a thick masala.
6. Meanwhile, bring a large pot of water to a boil with the cinnamon stick, cardamom pods, cloves, and bay leaf. Add the drained rice and boil 5–6 minutes, until the rice is about 70% cooked (still firm in the center). Drain immediately.
7. Soak the saffron strands in the warm milk for a few minutes until the milk turns golden.
8. Spread half the parboiled rice over the chicken masala in the pot. Sprinkle with half the fried onions, mint, and cilantro. Add the remaining rice on top, then drizzle with the saffron milk, the remaining oil, and the lemon juice. Finish with the rest of the fried onions, mint, and cilantro.
9. Cover the pot tightly with a lid (a layer of foil underneath helps seal in the steam). Cook on low heat for 20–25 minutes — this gentle steaming is called dum, and it's what lets the rice finish cooking in the fragrant steam from the chicken below.
10. Ask an adult to help you turn off the heat and let the pot rest, still covered, for 5–10 minutes. Gently fluff and mix just before serving so each spoonful gets rice, chicken, and crispy onion.

### 3. Seekh Kebab
Fragrant, hand-molded logs of spiced minced meat that go from raw to smoky-charred in minutes on a hot grill — juicy inside, crisp-edged outside, with a warm hit of roasted cumin and green chili in every bite.

| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground beef or lamb (not lean) | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Onion, grated and squeezed dry | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Ginger-garlic paste | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |
| Green chilies, minced | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Fresh cilantro, chopped | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Ground roasted cumin | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Garam masala | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Red chili powder | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Gram flour (besan), roasted | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | 3 tsp | 4 tsp |
| Salt | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Oil, for shaping and grilling | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Onion rings, to serve | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Lemon wedges, to serve | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

**Steps:**
1. Squeeze the grated onion firmly in a clean kitchen towel or paper towel to remove as much liquid as possible — a wet mixture won't hold together on the skewer.
2. In a large bowl, combine the ground meat, squeezed onion, ginger-garlic paste, minced green chilies, cilantro, roasted cumin, garam masala, red chili powder, roasted besan, salt, and black pepper.
3. Knead the mixture firmly with your hands for 2–3 minutes, until it feels sticky and holds together when pressed — this working of the meat is what keeps the kebabs from falling apart on the grill.
4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes; a chilled mixture is much easier to shape.
5. Ask an adult to help heat a grill, grill pan, or broiler to medium-high.
6. With oiled hands, take a portion of the mixture and mold it around a skewer into a long, even log about 2cm thick, pressing firmly so it grips the skewer.
7. Grill the kebabs, turning every couple of minutes, for 8–10 minutes total, until deeply charred in spots and cooked through with no pink in the center.
8. Serve hot, straight off the skewer, with onion rings and lemon wedges for squeezing over the top.

### 4. Chapli Kebab
Wide, craggy, hand-flattened patties studded with crushed coriander and cumin seeds and little bursts of tart pomegranate — fried in a hot pan until the edges turn deep mahogany and crackly-crisp while the inside stays juicy, spiced, and impossible to stop eating.

| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground beef | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Onion, finely chopped | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Tomato, finely chopped (seeds removed) | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Green chilies, chopped | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Coriander seeds, coarsely crushed | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |
| Cumin seeds, coarsely crushed | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Dried pomegranate seeds (anardana), crushed | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |
| Ginger-garlic paste | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |
| Red chili flakes | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Egg | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Cornflour or gram flour (besan) | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | 3 tsp | 4 tsp |
| Fresh cilantro, chopped | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Salt | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Oil, for shallow-frying | 30ml | 60ml | 90ml | 120ml |
| Tomato and onion slices, to serve | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |

**Steps:**
1. In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, chopped onion, chopped tomato, green chilies, crushed coriander seeds, crushed cumin seeds, crushed pomegranate seeds, ginger-garlic paste, and chili flakes.
2. Add the egg, cornflour or besan, chopped cilantro, and salt. Mix everything together well with your hands until evenly combined — the mixture will feel loose and wet; that's normal for chapli kebab.
3. Divide the mixture into portions and shape each one between your palms into a wide, flat patty about 1cm thick — traditionally these are gently slapped flat by hand rather than pressed into a neat, uniform circle, which is where the "chapli" (flat) name comes from.
4. Ask an adult to help heat the oil in a wide, heavy pan over medium heat until shimmering.
5. Carefully lay the patties in the hot oil, without crowding the pan, and fry 4–5 minutes per side, until deep reddish-brown and crisp at the edges and cooked through in the middle.
6. Drain briefly on paper towels.
7. Serve hot, topped with sliced tomato and onion.

### 5. Chicken Tikka
Bright red-gold cubes of chicken thigh, bathed overnight in yogurt, ginger, garlic, and warm spice, then blistered over high heat until the edges char dark and smoky while the inside stays impossibly juicy.

| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken thighs, boneless, cubed | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Plain yogurt | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Ginger-garlic paste | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |
| Lemon juice | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Red chili powder | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Ground turmeric | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Garam masala | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Ground cumin | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Vegetable oil | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Salt | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Onion rings, to serve | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Lemon wedges, to serve | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

**Steps:**
1. In a large bowl, whisk together the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, red chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, cumin, oil, and salt.
2. Add the cubed chicken thighs and turn to coat completely in the marinade. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour — overnight develops even deeper flavor.
3. If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for 20 minutes so they don't burn on the grill.
4. Thread the marinated chicken onto skewers, leaving a little space between pieces so they cook evenly.
5. Ask an adult to help heat a grill, grill pan, or broiler to high.
6. Cook the skewers 10–12 minutes total, turning every few minutes, until charred in spots and cooked through with no pink in the center.
7. Serve hot with onion rings and lemon wedges for squeezing over the top.

### 6. Daal Chawal
The everyday comfort dish of Pakistani kitchens: split red lentils simmered until silky and golden, then finished with a sizzling tarka of cumin seeds, garlic, and dried red chilies poured straight from the pan — you'll hear it crackle before you smell it. Ladled over fluffy white rice, it's warm, humble, and endlessly craveable.

| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Split red lentils (masoor daal) | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| White rice | 50g | 100g | 150g | 200g |
| Water (for cooking lentils) | 200ml | 400ml | 600ml | 800ml |
| Onion, chopped | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Tomato, chopped | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Garlic cloves, minced | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Ginger, minced | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Ground turmeric | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Red chili powder | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Cumin seeds (for tarka) | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Dried red chilies (for tarka) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Ghee or oil | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Salt | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Fresh cilantro, chopped | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |

**Steps:**
1. Rinse the lentils in a few changes of water until it runs mostly clear.
2. In a pot, combine the lentils, the measured water, the turmeric, and half the salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook, uncovered, 18–20 minutes, stirring occasionally and skimming off any foam, until the lentils are soft and starting to fall apart.
3. Meanwhile, cook the rice separately following the package directions and keep warm.
4. Ask an adult to help warm the ghee in a small pan over medium heat for the tarka. Add the cumin seeds and dried red chilies and let them sizzle for about 15 seconds, until fragrant and the chilies darken slightly.
5. Add the minced garlic and ginger to the tarka pan and cook 30 seconds, just until golden — watch closely, as garlic burns fast.
6. Add the chopped onion to the tarka pan and cook 4–5 minutes, until softened and lightly golden.
7. Stir in the chopped tomato and red chili powder and cook 3–4 minutes, until the tomato breaks down.
8. Pour the sizzling tarka straight into the pot of simmered lentils and stir well. Season with the remaining salt, then simmer together 3–4 more minutes so the flavors meld.
9. Sprinkle with chopped cilantro and serve hot, ladled over the warm rice.

### 7. Aloo Keema
Rustic, deeply spiced minced beef simmered down with soft, stew-tender potato chunks until every bite is coated in a thick, chili-red masala — the kind of one-pot weeknight dinner that tastes like it simmered all day.

| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground beef | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Potatoes, diced | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Onion, chopped | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Tomato, chopped | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Ginger-garlic paste | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |
| Green chilies, chopped | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Ground cumin | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Ground coriander | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Red chili powder | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Ground turmeric | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Garam masala | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Vegetable oil | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Water | 60ml | 120ml | 180ml | 240ml |
| Salt | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Fresh cilantro, chopped | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Roti, to serve | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

**Steps:**
1. Ask an adult to help warm the oil in a wide pan or pot over medium heat.
2. Add the chopped onion and cook 5–6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soft and golden at the edges.
3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and green chilies and cook 30 seconds, just until fragrant.
4. Add the chopped tomato, cumin, coriander, red chili powder, and turmeric. Cook 4–5 minutes, mashing the tomato with your spoon, until it breaks down into a thick paste.
5. Add the ground beef, breaking it up with your spoon, and cook 6–7 minutes, stirring often, until browned all over with no pink remaining.
6. Add the diced potatoes, the water, the garam masala, and the salt. Stir well, then cover and simmer 15–18 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the potatoes are fork-tender and the mixture has thickened into a clinging masala.
7. Uncover and simmer a few more minutes if the mixture still looks watery.
8. Taste and adjust salt if needed, then sprinkle with chopped cilantro.
9. Serve hot with warm roti for scooping.

### 8. Gulab Jamun (Bonus)
Golden-fried milk dumplings that turn deep mahogany-brown as they cook, then soak in warm cardamom-rose syrup until they're soft enough to practically melt on the tongue — sweet, fragrant, and a little bit magical the first time you make one at home.

| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Milk powder | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| All-purpose flour | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Baking powder | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Ghee, melted | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Milk (to bind dough) | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Sugar (for syrup) | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Water (for syrup) | 100ml | 200ml | 300ml | 400ml |
| Green cardamom pods | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Rosewater | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Oil or ghee, for deep-frying | 250ml | 500ml | 750ml | 1000ml |

**Steps:**
1. In a bowl, mix the milk powder, flour, and baking powder. Rub in the melted ghee with your fingertips until the mixture looks like damp sand.
2. Add the milk a little at a time, gently bringing the dough together — knead just until smooth; overworking it makes tough, cracked dumplings. Let it rest 10 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, make the syrup: ask an adult to help warm the sugar, water, and cardamom pods in a small pot over medium heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves. Simmer 5 minutes, then take off the heat and stir in the rosewater. Keep the syrup warm.
4. Divide the dough into small portions and roll each one between your palms into a smooth ball with no cracks — cracks will cause the dumplings to split open while frying.
5. Ask an adult to help heat the oil or ghee in a deep, heavy pot over medium-low heat — it should be gentle, not sizzling-hot, or the outside will brown before the inside cooks through.
6. Fry the balls in small batches, turning them often, for 8–10 minutes, until evenly deep reddish-brown all over.
7. Lift the fried dumplings straight into the warm syrup and let them soak for at least 1 hour, until they've plumped up and soaked the syrup all the way through.
8. Serve warm or at room temperature, spooning a little extra syrup over each one.
