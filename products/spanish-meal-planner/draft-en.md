## Recipe Cards

All ingredient tables below are scaled for 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 servings.

### 1. Paella Valenciana (Chicken & Shrimp)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bomba or short-grain paella rice | 65g | 130g | 195g | 260g |
| Chicken thighs, bone-in, cut into pieces | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Large shrimp, peeled and deveined, tail-on | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Chicken stock | 200ml | 400ml | 600ml | 800ml |
| Saffron threads | 8 threads | 16 threads | 24 threads | 32 threads |
| Olive oil | 8ml | 16ml | 24ml | 32ml |
| Onion, diced | 25g | 50g | 75g | 100g |
| Garlic cloves | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Roma tomato, grated (or crushed tomatoes) | 25g | 50g | 75g | 100g |
| Red bell pepper, sliced | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Sweet paprika | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Frozen peas | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Lemon wedges | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

**Steps:**
1. Steep the saffron threads in 2 tablespoons of warm stock for 10 minutes to bloom their color and aroma.
2. Season the chicken pieces with salt. Heat olive oil in a wide paella pan or large skillet over medium-high heat and brown the chicken 6–8 minutes, turning once, until deeply golden. Remove to a plate.
3. In the same pan, add the onion, garlic, and red pepper. Cook 5 minutes until softened.
4. Stir in the grated tomato and sweet paprika, cooking 3–4 minutes until jammy and slightly darker.
5. Add the rice and stir 1–2 minutes to coat every grain in the sofrito.
6. Return the chicken to the pan, pour in the stock and saffron mixture, and stir once to distribute everything evenly — after this point, do not stir again.
7. Simmer uncovered over medium heat 10 minutes, then reduce to low and cook 8–10 minutes more, until the rice is nearly tender and most of the liquid is absorbed.
8. Nestle the shrimp into the rice and scatter the peas over the top. Cook 5 minutes more, until the shrimp turn pink and opaque.
9. Raise the heat for the last 1–2 minutes to form a crispy bottom layer (the socarrat) — listen for a light crackling sound and pull it off the heat before it burns.
10. Remove from heat, cover loosely with foil, and let it rest 5 minutes before serving straight from the pan with lemon wedges.

### 2. Tortilla Española (Spanish Potato Omelette)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Yukon gold or waxy potatoes, peeled, thin-sliced | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Onion, thinly sliced | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Eggs | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Olive oil (for confit-frying; most is drained off) | 100ml | 200ml | 300ml | 400ml |
| Salt | to taste | to taste | to taste | to taste |

**Steps:**
1. Peel and thinly slice the potatoes (about 3–4mm thick); thinly slice the onion.
2. Heat the olive oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat. Add the potatoes and onion, and season with salt.
3. Confit gently, stirring occasionally, 20–25 minutes, until the potatoes are fully tender but not browned — they should break slightly when pressed with a spatula.
4. Drain the potatoes and onion in a colander set over a bowl, reserving the oil. Let them cool for 5 minutes.
5. Beat the eggs in a large bowl with a pinch of salt.
6. Fold the drained potato-onion mixture into the beaten eggs and let it sit 5 minutes so the potatoes soak up some egg.
7. Heat 1–2 tablespoons of the reserved oil in a small nonstick skillet (18–20cm) over medium heat.
8. Pour in the egg-potato mixture, spreading it evenly, and cook 2–3 minutes until the edges set.
9. Slide the tortilla onto a large plate, place the pan upside down on top, and flip both together so the tortilla drops back into the pan uncooked-side down — this is the trickiest step, so go slowly over the sink in case of spills. Cook the other side 2–3 minutes, until just set but still a little soft in the center.
10. Slide onto a plate, let rest 5 minutes, and slice into wedges to serve warm or at room temperature.

### 3. Gambas al Ajillo (Garlic Shrimp)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Large shrimp, peeled and deveined | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Olive oil | 30ml | 60ml | 90ml | 120ml |
| Garlic cloves, thinly sliced | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 |
| Dried chili flakes (or ¼ dried guindilla/arbol chile) | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Dry sherry (fino) or dry white wine | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Lemon juice | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |
| Fresh parsley, chopped | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Crusty bread, for serving | 1 slice | 2 slices | 3 slices | 4 slices |
| Salt | to taste | to taste | to taste | to taste |

**Steps:**
1. Pat the shrimp dry and season lightly with salt.
2. Combine the olive oil, sliced garlic, and chili flakes in a cold skillet.
3. Set the pan over medium-low heat so the garlic warms up gradually with the oil, 2–3 minutes, until fragrant and just turning golden at the edges — don't let it brown, or it turns bitter.
4. Raise the heat to medium-high and add the shrimp in a single layer.
5. Cook 1–2 minutes per side, until the shrimp turn pink and opaque.
6. Pour in the sherry and let it bubble and reduce by about half, roughly 30 seconds.
7. Squeeze in the lemon juice and season with salt.
8. Remove from heat and sprinkle with the chopped parsley.
9. Serve immediately, still sizzling in the pan, with crusty bread for dipping into the garlicky oil.

### 4. Pollo al Ajillo (Garlic Chicken)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bone-in chicken thighs, cut into pieces | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Olive oil | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Garlic cloves, whole, peeled | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 |
| Dry white wine | 30ml | 60ml | 90ml | 120ml |
| Bay leaf | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Fresh thyme sprig | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Fresh parsley, chopped | 3g | 6g | 9g | 12g |
| Salt and black pepper | to taste | to taste | to taste | to taste |

**Steps:**
1. Season the chicken pieces with salt and pepper.
2. Heat the olive oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Brown the chicken skin-side down 5–6 minutes until deep golden, then flip and brown the other side 4–5 minutes. Remove the chicken to a plate.
4. Lower the heat to medium-low and add the whole garlic cloves to the same pan.
5. Cook the garlic gently 3–4 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden and softened but not burnt.
6. Pour in the white wine, scraping up any browned bits stuck to the bottom of the pan.
7. Return the chicken to the pan along with the bay leaf and thyme sprig; bring to a simmer.
8. Cover partway and simmer 15–18 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through with no pink near the bone.
9. Uncover and simmer 2–3 minutes more to reduce the sauce slightly.
10. Sprinkle with chopped parsley and serve with crusty bread or roasted potatoes to soak up the garlicky pan juices.

### 5. Albóndigas en Salsa (Spanish Meatballs in Tomato Sauce)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground beef | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Ground pork | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Egg | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Dried breadcrumbs, soaked in milk | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Milk (for soaking breadcrumbs) | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Garlic clove, minced (for meatballs) | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Onion, finely grated (for meatballs) | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Fresh parsley, chopped | 3g | 6g | 9g | 12g |
| Ground cumin | pinch | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp |
| Sweet paprika | pinch | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp |
| Olive oil (for frying meatballs) | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Onion, finely diced (for sauce) | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Garlic cloves (for sauce) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Roasted red pepper, chopped | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Crushed tomatoes | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Dry white wine | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Bay leaf | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Stock (chicken or beef) | 30ml | 60ml | 90ml | 120ml |

**Steps:**
1. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk for 5 minutes until softened.
2. In a bowl, combine the ground beef, ground pork, soaked breadcrumbs, egg, minced garlic, grated onion, parsley, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper. Mix gently with your hands until just combined — overmixing makes tough meatballs.
3. Roll into golf-ball-sized meatballs and chill 15–30 minutes to firm up.
4. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Brown the meatballs in batches, 2–3 minutes per side, until golden all over (they don't need to be cooked through yet). Remove to a plate.
5. In the same pan, cook the diced onion 4–5 minutes until soft.
6. Add the garlic and roasted red pepper, cooking 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
7. Pour in the white wine and let it reduce by half, scraping up any browned bits.
8. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, stock, and bay leaf; bring to a simmer.
9. Return the meatballs to the sauce, cover partway, and simmer 20–25 minutes, turning occasionally, until the meatballs are cooked through and the sauce has thickened.
10. Taste and adjust the salt, then discard the bay leaf.
11. Serve the meatballs and sauce with crusty bread or over rice.

### 6. Fabada Asturiana (Bean & Chorizo Stew)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dried large white beans (fabes, or gigante/butter beans), soaked overnight | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Spanish chorizo (cured, for cooking) | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Morcilla (Spanish blood sausage) | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Pork belly or salt pork (tocino) | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Garlic cloves | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Onion | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Bay leaf | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Smoked paprika (pimentón) | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Saffron threads | pinch | pinch | small pinch | pinch |
| Stock or water | 300ml | 600ml | 900ml | 1200ml |
| Salt | to taste | to taste | to taste | to taste |

**Steps:**
1. Soak the dried beans in plenty of cold water overnight, at least 8 hours; drain before cooking.
2. Put the soaked beans in a large pot with the chorizo, tocino, whole garlic cloves, onion, and bay leaf.
3. Cover generously with cold stock or water (the beans should be submerged by a couple of inches) and bring slowly to a bare simmer over medium heat — never let it boil hard, or the bean skins split.
4. Skim off any foam that rises to the top.
5. Stir in the saffron and smoked paprika, partially cover, and simmer very gently 1.5–2 hours, until the beans are completely tender and creamy. If the pot threatens to boil too hard, add a splash of cold water to calm it back down ("asustar las fabes," or "scaring the beans").
6. About 20 minutes before the beans are done, add the morcilla so it doesn't fall apart from overcooking.
7. Once tender, lift out the chorizo, morcilla, and tocino, slice, and return to the pot.
8. Season the broth with salt to taste — it should be thick enough to lightly coat a spoon, not watery.
9. Let the stew rest off the heat for 10 minutes before serving so the flavors settle.
10. Ladle into bowls, making sure everyone gets beans, broth, and a bit of each meat, with crusty bread on the side.

### 7. Patatas Bravas with Alioli (Dinner Combo, with Crusty Bread)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Yukon gold or russet potatoes, 2cm cubes | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Olive oil (for frying/roasting potatoes) | 30ml | 60ml | 90ml | 120ml |
| Onion, finely diced (for brava sauce) | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Garlic cloves (for brava sauce) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Crushed tomatoes | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Smoked paprika | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Cayenne pepper | pinch | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp |
| Sherry vinegar | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Olive oil (for brava sauce) | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Garlic clove (for alioli) | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Egg yolk | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Olive oil (for alioli) | 30ml | 60ml | 90ml | 120ml |
| Lemon juice | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Crusty bread, for serving | 1 slice | 2 slices | 3 slices | 4 slices |

**Steps:**
1. Soak the cubed potatoes in cold water 20–30 minutes to remove excess starch, then drain and pat completely dry.
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet (or heat the oven to 220°C/425°F). Fry or roast the potatoes, turning occasionally, 20–25 minutes, until deeply golden and crisp outside, tender inside.
3. Meanwhile, make the brava sauce: warm olive oil in a small saucepan, add the onion, and cook 4–5 minutes until soft.
4. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute until fragrant, then stir in the smoked paprika and cayenne, cooking 30 seconds to bloom the spices.
5. Add the crushed tomatoes and simmer 10–12 minutes until thickened; stir in the sherry vinegar and season with salt.
6. For the alioli, whisk the minced garlic with the egg yolk and a pinch of salt in a bowl.
7. Whisking constantly, add the oil in a very thin, slow stream at first, then a little faster once it starts to thicken, until you have a thick, glossy, mayonnaise-like sauce.
8. Whisk in the lemon juice and season with salt to taste.
9. Drain the crisped potatoes on paper towels and pile onto a plate.
10. Spoon the warm brava sauce generously over the potatoes and drizzle with the alioli.
11. Serve immediately with crusty bread alongside, as a full dinner-for-one combo plate.

### 8. Crema Catalana (Bonus)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whole milk | 125ml | 250ml | 375ml | 500ml |
| Egg yolks | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Granulated sugar (for custard) | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Cornstarch | 4g | 8g | 12g | 16g |
| Lemon peel (strip, no pith) | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Orange peel (strip, no pith) | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Cinnamon stick | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Granulated sugar (for caramelized top) | 8g | 15g | 23g | 30g |

**Steps:**
1. Pour the milk into a saucepan with the lemon peel, orange peel, and cinnamon stick.
2. Warm gently over medium heat until steaming but not boiling, then remove from heat and let steep 10–15 minutes to infuse. Strain out the peels and cinnamon stick.
3. In a bowl, whisk the egg yolks with the sugar until pale, then whisk in the cornstarch until smooth with no lumps.
4. Slowly pour a little of the warm infused milk into the yolk mixture, whisking constantly to temper the eggs.
5. Pour the tempered mixture back into the saucepan with the rest of the milk.
6. Cook over low heat, whisking constantly, 5–8 minutes, until the custard thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon.
7. Pour the custard into shallow ramekins or traditional terracotta dishes and let cool to room temperature.
8. Cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours, until fully chilled and set.
9. Just before serving, sprinkle an even layer of sugar over the top of each custard.
10. Caramelize the sugar with a kitchen torch (or under a hot broiler for 1–2 minutes) until it forms a deep amber, crackly crust.
11. Serve immediately while the crust is still crisp.
