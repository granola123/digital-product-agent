### 1. Mercimek Çorbası (Red Lentil Soup)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Red lentils | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Onion, chopped | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Carrot, chopped | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Potato, chopped | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Garlic clove | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Tomato paste | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Ground cumin | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Paprika | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Chicken or vegetable stock | 250ml | 500ml | 750ml | 1000ml |
| Olive oil | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Butter (for the tarka) | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Dried mint | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Pul biber (Aleppo pepper flakes) | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Salt | pinch | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Lemon wedges | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

**Steps:**
1. Warm the olive oil in a pot over medium heat. Add the onion and carrot and cook 4–5 minutes until softened.
2. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Stir in the tomato paste, cumin, and paprika, cooking 1 minute until deep red and fragrant.
4. Add the red lentils, potato, and stock. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer 20–25 minutes until the lentils and potato are completely soft and falling apart.
5. Blend the soup until smooth and velvety, using an immersion blender or a standard blender in batches.
6. Return to low heat and thin with a splash of water or stock if needed. Season with salt.
7. Make the tarka: melt the butter in a small pan, add the dried mint and pul biber, and sizzle 20–30 seconds until fragrant — watch closely so it doesn't burn.
8. Ladle the soup into bowls, drizzle the mint–pul biber butter on top, and serve with lemon wedges and a pinch more pul biber on the side.

### 2. Turkish Köfte (Spiced Meatballs) with Rice Pilaf
**Köfte — Ingredients by Serving**
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground beef (or beef & lamb mix) | 120g | 240g | 360g | 480g |
| Onion, grated | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Garlic clove, grated | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Dried breadcrumbs | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Ground cumin | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Paprika | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Dried oregano | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Parsley, chopped | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Black pepper | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Olive oil (for searing) | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |

**Rice Pilaf — Ingredients by Serving**
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baldo or long-grain rice | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Orzo (şehriye) | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Butter | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Olive oil | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Chicken stock | 120ml | 240ml | 360ml | 480ml |
| Salt | pinch | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp |

**Steps:**
1. In a bowl, combine the ground beef, grated onion, garlic, breadcrumbs, cumin, paprika, oregano, parsley, salt, and pepper. Mix and knead briefly until well combined and slightly sticky.
2. Shape into oval or flattened round patties about the size of your palm. Rest 10 minutes in the fridge if time allows — this helps them hold together.
3. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
4. Sear the köfte 3–4 minutes per side until deeply browned and cooked through with no pink in the center.
5. Meanwhile, make the pilaf: melt the butter with the olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the orzo and toast 1–2 minutes, stirring, until golden brown.
6. Add the rice and stir to coat every grain in the fat, toasting 1–2 minutes more.
7. Pour in the hot chicken stock and salt, bring to a boil, then cover, reduce heat to low, and simmer 15 minutes without lifting the lid.
8. Remove from heat, keep covered, and let rest 5–10 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
9. Serve the köfte hot alongside the rice pilaf.

### 3. Karnıyarık (Stuffed Eggplant with Ground Beef)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Eggplants, medium | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Ground beef | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Onion, chopped | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Garlic cloves, minced | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Green pepper, chopped | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Tomatoes, diced | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Tomato paste | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Red pepper paste (biber salçası) | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Parsley, chopped | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Ground cumin | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Salt | pinch | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Black pepper | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Olive oil (for frying) | 40ml | 80ml | 120ml | 160ml |
| Water or stock | 30ml | 60ml | 90ml | 120ml |
| Tomato slices (for topping) | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |

**Steps:**
1. Peel the eggplants in alternating strips (leaving some skin for structure), then slit each lengthwise down the middle without cutting all the way through, forming a pocket.
2. Salt the eggplants and let sit 15 minutes to draw out bitterness, then rinse and pat dry.
3. Shallow-fry the eggplants in olive oil over medium heat, turning, until softened and lightly golden all over, about 8–10 minutes. Drain and arrange in a baking dish, gently pressing open the slit.
4. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
5. In the same pan, cook the onion 3–4 minutes until soft, then add the garlic and green pepper, cooking 2 minutes more.
6. Add the ground beef and cook, breaking it up, until browned with no pink remaining.
7. Stir in the tomato paste and red pepper paste, cooking 1 minute until deep red and fragrant.
8. Add the diced tomatoes, cumin, parsley, salt, and pepper, and simmer 5 minutes until slightly thickened.
9. Spoon the meat filling generously into the slit of each eggplant, mounding it on top.
10. Top each stuffed eggplant with a tomato slice and a strip of green pepper. Pour the water or stock into the bottom of the baking dish.
11. Bake 20–25 minutes until the eggplant is fully tender and the filling is bubbling.
12. Serve hot, with rice pilaf and yogurt on the side if you like.

### 4. Tavuk Şiş (Chicken Shish Kebab)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken thighs, boneless & skinless, cubed | 130g | 260g | 390g | 520g |
| Plain yogurt | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Olive oil | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Tomato paste | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Garlic clove, grated | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Paprika | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Ground cumin | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Pul biber (Aleppo pepper flakes) | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Dried oregano | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Salt | pinch | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Bell pepper (for skewers) | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Cherry tomatoes (for skewers) | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Red onion, thinly sliced | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Sumac | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Lemon wedges | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

**Steps:**
1. In a bowl, whisk together the yogurt, olive oil, tomato paste, garlic, paprika, cumin, pul biber, oregano, salt, and pepper.
2. Add the cubed chicken and toss until every piece is coated. Cover and marinate in the fridge at least 1 hour (overnight for deeper flavor).
3. Thread the marinated chicken onto skewers, alternating with pieces of bell pepper and cherry tomatoes.
4. Heat a grill, grill pan, or broiler to high heat.
5. Grill the skewers 10–12 minutes total, turning every few minutes, until the chicken is deeply charred in spots and cooked through with no pink at the center.
6. While the chicken grills, toss the sliced red onion with sumac and a pinch of salt for a quick onion salad.
7. Serve the skewers hot with the sumac onion salad and lemon wedges for squeezing over.

### 5. Turkish Chicken Güveç (Chicken & Vegetable Stew)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken thighs, boneless & skinless, chunked | 130g | 260g | 390g | 520g |
| Onion, sliced | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Garlic cloves | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Red bell pepper, chopped | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Green bell pepper, chopped | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Potato, chopped | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Tomatoes, chopped | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Tomato paste | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Red pepper paste (biber salçası) | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Bay leaf | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Dried oregano | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Dried mint | pinch | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp |
| Salt | pinch | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Black pepper | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Olive oil | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Water or stock | 60ml | 120ml | 180ml | 240ml |

**Steps:**
1. Warm the olive oil in a wide pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the chicken and sear 3–4 minutes until lightly golden (it won't be cooked through yet).
2. Add the onion and garlic, cooking 3–4 minutes until softened.
3. Stir in the tomato paste and red pepper paste, cooking 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add the bell peppers, potato, tomatoes, bay leaf, oregano, dried mint, salt, and pepper.
5. Pour in the water or stock, stir well, and bring to a gentle simmer.
6. Cover and simmer on low heat 35–40 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is fully cooked and the potatoes are tender.
7. Uncover and simmer 5–10 more minutes to thicken the sauce slightly.
8. Discard the bay leaf, taste and adjust seasoning, then serve hot straight from the pot with crusty bread. (Traditionally baked in a clay güveç pot in the oven for 1½ hours — this stovetop version gets the same flavors onto the table on a weeknight schedule.)

### 6. Lahmacun (Turkish Flatbread with Spiced Meat)
**Dough — Ingredients by Serving**
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bread or all-purpose flour | 70g | 140g | 210g | 280g |
| Instant yeast | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Warm water | 40ml | 80ml | 120ml | 160ml |
| Olive oil | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Salt | pinch | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |

**Topping — Ingredients by Serving**
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground lamb or beef | 80g | 160g | 240g | 320g |
| Onion, finely chopped | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Tomato, finely chopped | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Garlic clove | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Parsley, finely chopped | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Tomato paste | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Red pepper paste (biber salçası) | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Paprika | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Pul biber | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Ground cumin | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |

**Steps:**
1. Make the dough: dissolve the yeast in the warm water and let sit 5 minutes until foamy.
2. Combine the flour and salt in a bowl, add the yeast water and olive oil, and knead 5–6 minutes until smooth and elastic.
3. Cover and let rise in a warm spot 20–25 minutes until slightly puffy — it won't fully double, and that's fine for lahmacun's thin crust.
4. Meanwhile, make the topping: in a food processor or by hand, finely chop the onion, tomato, garlic, and parsley together until they form a wet, finely minced paste.
5. Combine this vegetable mixture with the ground lamb, tomato paste, red pepper paste, paprika, pul biber, cumin, salt, and pepper, mixing until the meat mixture is smooth and spreadable.
6. Preheat the oven as hot as it goes (240–260°C / 465–500°F) with a baking sheet or pizza stone inside to heat up.
7. Divide the dough into portions and roll each one out very thin — almost paper-thin — on a floured surface.
8. Spread a thin, even layer of the meat topping all the way to the edges of each dough round.
9. Carefully transfer the topped rounds onto the hot baking sheet and bake 7–9 minutes until the edges are crisp and lightly browned and the meat is fully cooked.
10. Serve immediately, rolled up with a squeeze of lemon, fresh parsley, and sliced onion inside.

### 7. Turkish Pide (Cheese & Beef Flatbread)
**Dough — Ingredients by Serving**
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bread flour | 90g | 180g | 270g | 360g |
| Instant yeast | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Warm water | 50ml | 100ml | 150ml | 200ml |
| Plain yogurt | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Olive oil | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Sugar | pinch | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Salt | pinch | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |

**Filling — Ingredients by Serving**
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground beef | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Onion, finely chopped | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Green pepper, chopped | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Tomato, chopped | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Tomato paste | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Red pepper paste (biber salçası) | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Shredded kaşar cheese (or mozzarella) | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Paprika | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Pul biber | pinch | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp |
| Egg (for egg wash) | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Butter, melted (to finish) | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |

**Steps:**
1. Dissolve the yeast and sugar in the warm water; let sit 5 minutes until foamy.
2. Mix in the yogurt, olive oil, flour, and salt, and knead 6–8 minutes until smooth and elastic.
3. Cover and let rise in a warm place 45–60 minutes until roughly doubled (or 25–30 minutes in a slightly warmed oven if you're short on time).
4. Meanwhile, mix the ground beef, onion, green pepper, tomato, tomato paste, red pepper paste, paprika, pul biber, salt, and pepper in a bowl to make the filling.
5. Preheat the oven to 230°C (450°F) with a baking sheet inside.
6. Punch down the dough and roll each portion into a long oval, about 1cm thick.
7. Spread the meat filling down the center, leaving a 2–3cm border, and sprinkle the shredded kaşar cheese over the filling.
8. Fold the two long edges of the dough up and over the filling, pinching the ends together tightly to form a boat shape that keeps the filling visible down the middle.
9. Brush the exposed crust with beaten egg for shine.
10. Slide the pide onto the hot baking sheet and bake 12–15 minutes until the crust is deep golden and the filling is bubbling and cooked through.
11. Brush the hot crust with melted butter as soon as it comes out of the oven, then slice and serve.

### 8. Sütlaç (Turkish Rice Pudding) (Bonus)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short or medium-grain rice | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Water (to par-cook rice) | 60ml | 120ml | 180ml | 240ml |
| Whole milk | 200ml | 400ml | 600ml | 800ml |
| Sugar | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Cornstarch | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Vanilla extract | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Egg yolk (optional, for richness) | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Ground cinnamon (for topping) | pinch | ⅛ tsp | ¼ tsp | ⅜ tsp |

**Steps:**
1. Rinse the rice and simmer it in the water over low heat, covered, 10–12 minutes until just tender and the water is mostly absorbed.
2. Add the milk to the pot and bring to a gentle simmer, stirring occasionally so the rice doesn't stick to the bottom.
3. In a small bowl, whisk the cornstarch with a splash of cold milk until smooth, then stir this slurry into the pot.
4. Add the sugar and simmer 10–15 minutes, stirring frequently, until the mixture thickens to a thick, pourable custard consistency.
5. Take a spoonful of the hot mixture and whisk it into the egg yolk to temper it, then stir the tempered yolk back into the pot (skip this for an eggless version — the pudding still sets from the cornstarch).
6. Stir in the vanilla extract and simmer 2 more minutes.
7. Pour the pudding into individual ramekins or bowls.
8. For the traditional caramelized top, place the ramekins under a hot broiler for 2–3 minutes until brown spots form on the surface, watching closely so it doesn't burn — or skip this and simply dust with cinnamon.
9. Let cool to room temperature, then refrigerate at least 2 hours until fully chilled and set.
10. Sprinkle with ground cinnamon just before serving.
