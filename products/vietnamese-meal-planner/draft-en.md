## Grocery List (by category)

**Produce & Herbs:** yellow onion, garlic, fresh ginger, shallots, scallion, cilantro, Thai basil, mint, Vietnamese perilla (tía tô), limes, red Thai chili, jalapeño, lettuce, watercress, tomato, cucumber, carrot, daikon radish, fresh pineapple, okra, bean sprouts, rice paddy herb (ngò om), jicama (or shredded cabbage), lemongrass

**Protein & Seafood:** bone-in chicken thighs (for phở and kho gừng), ground pork, pork belly or shoulder (for bún chả), pork shoulder or loin (for bánh mì), beef sirloin, shrimp, white fish steaks such as catfish (for canh chua), eggs

**Noodles, Rice & Wrappers:** dried flat rice noodles (bánh phở), rice vermicelli (bún), rice paper wrappers, dried glass (cellophane) noodles, baguettes, jasmine rice

**Sauces & Pantry:** fish sauce, soy sauce, oyster sauce, hoisin sauce, granulated sugar, rock sugar (or extra granulated sugar), star anise, whole cloves, coriander seeds, cinnamon stick, dried wood ear mushroom, tamarind pulp (or paste), Maggi seasoning, mayonnaise, pork liver pâté (optional), roasted peanuts, sesame oil, vegetable oil, rice vinegar

**Dairy & Eggs:** whole milk, butter, vanilla extract, eggs (also listed under protein for chả giò's binder and bánh flan's custard)

## Recipe Cards

All ingredient tables below are scaled for 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 servings.

### 1. Phở Gà (Chicken Pho)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken thighs, bone-in | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Water | 500ml | 1000ml | 1500ml | 2000ml |
| Yellow onion | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Fresh ginger | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Star anise | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Whole cloves | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Coriander seeds | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Cinnamon stick | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Fish sauce | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Rock sugar | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Dried flat rice noodles | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Bean sprouts | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Thai basil | 3g | 6g | 9g | 12g |
| Scallion, sliced | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Cilantro | 3g | 6g | 9g | 12g |
| Lime wedges | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Red chili, sliced | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |

**Steps:**
1. Char the onion and ginger directly over a gas flame or under a broiler, turning until blackened in spots, then rinse off the loose char under running water.
2. In a dry pot, toast the star anise, cloves, coriander seeds, and cinnamon stick over medium heat 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
3. Add the chicken thighs, water, and charred onion and ginger. Bring to a boil, then skim off any grey foam.
4. Reduce heat and simmer, partially covered, 30-35 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
5. Lift out the chicken, let it cool slightly, then shred or slice the meat off the bone.
6. Strain the broth through a fine sieve, discarding the solids. Return the clear broth to the pot and season with fish sauce and rock sugar to taste.
7. Soak the rice noodles in hot water 10-15 minutes until softened, then blanch briefly in boiling water and drain.
8. Divide the noodles into bowls and top with the shredded chicken.
9. Ladle the hot broth over each bowl.
10. Top with bean sprouts, Thai basil, scallion, cilantro, chili, and a lime wedge.

### 2. Bún Chả (Grilled Pork with Vermicelli & Herbs)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground pork | 100g | 200g | 300g | 400g |
| Pork belly or shoulder, thin-sliced | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Garlic, minced | 1 clove | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Shallot, minced | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Fish sauce | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Sugar | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Black pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Rice vermicelli | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Lettuce | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Mixed herbs (mint, perilla, Thai basil) | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Pickled carrot & daikon (đồ chua) | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Nuoc cham (fish sauce, sugar, lime, water, garlic, chili) | 60ml | 120ml | 180ml | 240ml |

**Steps:**
1. Mix the ground pork with half the garlic, shallot, fish sauce, sugar, and pepper, then shape into small flat patties. Toss the sliced pork belly with the remaining garlic, shallot, fish sauce, and sugar.
2. Cover both and refrigerate at least 30 minutes.
3. Whisk together the nuoc cham: fish sauce, sugar, lime juice, water, minced garlic, and chili.
4. Julienne the carrot and daikon and toss with a little vinegar, sugar, and salt; let sit 15 minutes to quick-pickle.
5. Grill or broil the patties and pork belly slices 3-4 minutes per side until charred at the edges and cooked through.
6. Slide the warm grilled pork into a bowl of the nuoc cham while it's still hot.
7. Cook the vermicelli noodles according to the package, then rinse under cold water and drain.
8. Serve the noodles, lettuce, and herbs alongside the bowl of pork in nuoc cham, topped with the pickled carrot and daikon.
9. To eat, dip noodles and herbs into the sauce bowl bite by bite alongside the pork.

### 3. Bánh Mì Thịt Nướng (Grilled Pork Banh Mi)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pork shoulder or loin, thin-sliced | 120g | 240g | 360g | 480g |
| Lemongrass, minced | 1 stalk | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Garlic, minced | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Shallot, minced | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Fish sauce | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |
| Sugar | 8g | 16g | 24g | 32g |
| Soy sauce | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Sesame oil | 3ml | 6ml | 9ml | 12ml |
| Baguette | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Pork liver pâté (optional) | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Mayonnaise | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Maggi seasoning (or soy sauce) | a few drops | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Pickled carrot & daikon (đồ chua) | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Cucumber, sliced | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Cilantro | 3g | 6g | 9g | 12g |
| Jalapeño or Thai chili | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |

**Steps:**
1. Combine the pork slices with lemongrass, garlic, shallot, fish sauce, sugar, soy sauce, and sesame oil.
2. Marinate at least 30 minutes (overnight in the fridge tastes best).
3. Heat a grill pan or broiler until very hot. Cook the pork slices 2-3 minutes per side until charred at the edges and caramelized.
4. Split the baguette lengthwise and toast lightly.
5. Spread mayonnaise and pâté inside both halves.
6. Drizzle a few drops of Maggi seasoning over the bread.
7. Layer the grilled pork inside.
8. Top with pickled carrot and daikon, cucumber, cilantro, and sliced chili.
9. Press the sandwich closed and slice in half to serve.

### 4. Bò Lúc Lắc (Shaking Beef)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beef sirloin, cubed | 130g | 260g | 390g | 520g |
| Soy sauce | 8ml | 16ml | 24ml | 32ml |
| Oyster sauce | 8ml | 16ml | 24ml | 32ml |
| Fish sauce | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Sugar | 3g | 6g | 9g | 12g |
| Garlic, minced | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Black pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Butter | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Red onion, sliced | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Watercress (or lettuce) | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Tomato, sliced | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Cucumber, sliced | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Lime juice (for dipping salt) | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Salt & pepper (for dipping) | pinch | pinch | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp |

**Steps:**
1. Marinate the beef cubes with soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce, sugar, garlic, and pepper for 20-30 minutes.
2. Heat a wok or heavy skillet until very hot with a little oil.
3. Sear the beef in a single layer, working in batches, 30-45 seconds per side until browned but still pink in the center.
4. Add the butter and sliced onion, tossing 1-2 minutes until the onion softens slightly and the beef looks glossy.
5. Arrange the watercress, tomato, and cucumber on a plate.
6. Pile the beef and onion on top.
7. Stir the lime juice with a pinch of salt and pepper to make the dipping sauce.
8. Serve immediately with steamed rice and the lime-salt-pepper dip.

### 5. Gỏi Cuốn & Chả Giò (Fresh & Fried Spring Rolls)

**Gỏi Cuốn Ingredients by Serving**

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rice paper wrappers | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Rice vermicelli noodles | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Shrimp, cooked & halved lengthwise | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 |
| Pork belly, boiled & thinly sliced | 40g | 80g | 120g | 160g |
| Lettuce leaves | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Fresh herbs (mint, perilla) | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Peanut hoisin dipping sauce | 30ml | 60ml | 90ml | 120ml |

**Chả Giò Ingredients by Serving**
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground pork | 60g | 120g | 180g | 240g |
| Shrimp, chopped | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Dried wood ear mushroom, soaked & minced | 5g | 10g | 15g | 20g |
| Dried glass noodles, soaked & chopped | 10g | 20g | 30g | 40g |
| Carrot, grated | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Jicama (or shredded cabbage) | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Garlic & shallot, minced | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 1½ tsp | 2 tsp |
| Egg, beaten (binder) | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |
| Fish sauce | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Black pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Rice paper or spring roll wrappers | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Vegetable oil (for frying) | 100ml | 200ml | 300ml | 400ml |
| Nuoc cham (for dipping) | 30ml | 60ml | 90ml | 120ml |

**Steps:**
1. For the chả giò, mix the ground pork, chopped shrimp, wood ear mushroom, glass noodles, carrot, jicama, garlic, shallot, egg, a splash of fish sauce, and a pinch of pepper into a filling.
2. Spoon filling onto a wrapper, fold in the sides, and roll tightly into a cigar shape; repeat with the remaining wrappers.
3. Heat the oil in a pot to about 170°C (340°F). Fry the rolls in batches 5-7 minutes, turning occasionally, until deep golden and crisp. Drain on paper towels.
4. Meanwhile for the gỏi cuốn, cook the rice vermicelli according to the package directions and drain.
5. Dip a rice paper wrapper in warm water 10-15 seconds until just pliable.
6. Lay lettuce, herbs, vermicelli, and pork belly slices near the bottom edge of the wrapper.
7. Place the shrimp halves cut-side up just above the filling so they show through the wrapper once rolled.
8. Fold the bottom of the wrapper over the filling, fold in the sides, and roll up tightly; repeat with the remaining wrappers.
9. Serve the fresh rolls with peanut hoisin sauce and the fried rolls with nuoc cham.

### 6. Gà Kho Gừng (Ginger Caramelized Chicken)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken thighs or drumsticks, bone-in | 150g | 300g | 450g | 600g |
| Fish sauce | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Soy sauce | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Garlic, minced | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Black pepper | pinch | ¼ tsp | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp |
| Sugar (for caramel) | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Water (to stop the caramel) | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Fresh ginger, julienned | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Shallots, sliced | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Vegetable oil | 5ml | 10ml | 15ml | 20ml |
| Scallion, sliced (garnish) | 3g | 6g | 9g | 12g |

**Steps:**
1. Marinate the chicken with fish sauce, soy sauce, half the garlic, and pepper for 15-20 minutes.
2. Make the caramel: melt the sugar in a dry pan over medium heat until it turns a deep amber, then carefully add the water off the heat to stop it cooking (it will bubble vigorously).
3. Add the oil, remaining garlic, ginger, and shallots to the caramel and stir until fragrant.
4. Add the marinated chicken and stir to coat evenly in the caramel; sear 2-3 minutes per side until golden.
5. Add a splash of water to just cover the bottom of the pan, cover, and simmer 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is cooked through.
6. Uncover and let the sauce reduce 3-5 minutes more, until syrupy and clinging to the chicken.
7. Garnish with scallion and serve hot with steamed rice.

### 7. Canh Chua (Sweet & Sour Fish Soup)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| White fish steaks (catfish or similar) | 130g | 260g | 390g | 520g |
| Tamarind pulp (or paste) | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Water | 300ml | 600ml | 900ml | 1200ml |
| Pineapple, chunked | 30g | 60g | 90g | 120g |
| Tomato, wedged | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 2 |
| Okra, sliced | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Bean sprouts | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Rice paddy herb (ngò om), chopped | 3g | 6g | 9g | 12g |
| Fish sauce | 15ml | 30ml | 45ml | 60ml |
| Sugar | 8g | 16g | 24g | 32g |
| Garlic, minced | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Fried shallots (garnish) | 3g | 6g | 9g | 12g |
| Thai chili, sliced | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1 |

**Steps:**
1. Soak the tamarind pulp in a little warm water, mash, and strain to make tamarind water (or dissolve tamarind paste directly into the broth).
2. Sauté the garlic in a splash of oil in a pot until fragrant.
3. Add the tamarind water and remaining water; bring to a boil.
4. Season with fish sauce and sugar, tasting and adjusting until the broth is balanced sour-sweet-salty.
5. Add the fish steaks and simmer gently 6-8 minutes until just cooked through.
6. Add the pineapple, tomato, and okra; simmer 3-4 minutes until just tender.
7. Stir in the bean sprouts and rice paddy herb right at the end so they stay crisp and fragrant.
8. Ladle into bowls and top with fried shallots and sliced chili.

### 8. Bánh Flan (Vietnamese Crème Caramel) (Bonus)
| Ingredient | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Eggs | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Whole milk | 80ml | 160ml | 240ml | 320ml |
| Sugar (for custard) | 15g | 30g | 45g | 60g |
| Vanilla extract | ¼ tsp | ½ tsp | ¾ tsp | 1 tsp |
| Sugar (for caramel) | 20g | 40g | 60g | 80g |
| Water (for caramel) | 10ml | 20ml | 30ml | 40ml |

**Steps:**
1. Make the caramel: melt the sugar with the water in a small pan over medium heat, swirling (not stirring) until it turns a deep amber.
2. Immediately pour the hot caramel into ramekins, tilting to coat the bottom, and let it harden.
3. Whisk the eggs, milk, sugar, and vanilla together until just combined — don't over-whip, or the custard will bake with bubbles.
4. Strain the custard through a fine sieve into the caramel-lined ramekins.
5. Cover with foil, set the ramekins in a baking dish, and pour hot water halfway up their sides (a bain-marie).
6. Bake at 150°C (300°F) for 30-35 minutes, until the custard is just set with a slight jiggle in the center.
7. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate at least 4 hours.
8. Run a knife around the edge of each ramekin and invert onto a plate to release the caramel sauce over the flan.
